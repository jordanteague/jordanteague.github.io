Jordan Teague

Lawyer, founder, and builder of legal AI systems.

I co-founded Campbell Teague, a Chambers-ranked firm that advises emerging-technology organizations on questions without settled answers, from token issuance under MiCA to decentralized protocol governance.

I also build the tools. Most of my recent work turns legal judgment into something AI agents can apply consistently: clearance searches, discovery review, and the playbooks behind them.

Building

  • TMrex

    Screening-level U.S. trademark clearance as a Claude skill. Searches a twice-daily-synced copy of the full USPTO register, about 14 million case files, plus the web and domain names for unregistered use, then reports on registrability.

  • ROGbuster

    Reviews the other side's discovery responses, flags every gap, and drafts the meet-and-confer letter.

  • Judgment Compiler

    A platform-neutral specification and Claude skill that compile a lawyer's judgment, from an interview or a starting draft, into schema-validated playbooks for AI review agents.

  • TrademarkBench pilot

    Can Claude predict how the USPTO will examine a trademark application? Tested on 90 applications examined after the model's training cutoff, with and without register search.

Before AI coding tools, I shipped iOS and SaaS apps and contributed Solidity contracts and front-end code to LexDAO, a guild of lawyers and developers. More on GitHub.
